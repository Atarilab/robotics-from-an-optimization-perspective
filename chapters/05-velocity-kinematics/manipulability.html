What am I looking at?

Jₚ maps joint rates to the velocity of the tool origin in the world x-y plane. The eigenvectors of JₚJₚᵀ give the shared principal directions. Velocity semiaxes have lengths σᵢ; force semiaxes have lengths 1/σᵢ when Jₚ has full rank.

Rotate q₁ while holding q₂ fixed: the directions rotate, but the semiaxis lengths do not change. Move q₂ to 0° or ±180°: the velocity ellipse collapses to a segment, while the ideal force set becomes a strip.

The bounds are Euclidean balls, not independent joint limits. Forces obey FᵀJₚJₚᵀF ≤ 1. At a singularity this inequality leaves a reaction direction unbounded; it does not describe unlimited strength or active force control. Gravity, inertia, contact, and structural limits are outside this example.
